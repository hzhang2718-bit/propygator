Extended validation -- Swarm energy-step maneuver probe
==============================================================================
[versions] propygator 0.8.0, numpy 2.4.6
[what]  one-revolution-mean semi-major axis from the delivered SP3 truth.
  Drag removes energy monotonically, so a STEP is a maneuver, a data
  defect, or nothing -- it is the one thing drag cannot produce.
[why]   the degree-5 polynomial is this leg's only gate, and it
  normalizes by the peak along-track signal, so a maneuver big enough
  to inflate that signal hides inside its own denominator.
[limits]  ENERGY only -- a pure cross-track or inclination burn is
  invisible here, and a bad POD day could mimic a step. This does not
  replace the polynomial, which catches safe-mode and attitude events.
[detection]  robust |z| > 20 on the one-revolution change in
  the smoothed a, median/MAD scale. Pre-registered, NOT tuned: clean
  windows top out near 4 sigma and the detection sits above 250, so any
  cut from 10 to 100 gives the identical verdict.
[scope]  the 8 loaded days of each frozen window, at the native
  10 s grid -- exactly the span Part 2's drag rows rest on.
