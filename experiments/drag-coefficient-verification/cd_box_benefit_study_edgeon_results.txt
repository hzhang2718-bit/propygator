RESULTS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
BoxFace altitude: 400.0 -> 386.9 km over 5 days

1. PRIMARY -- along-track divergence a real user suffers vs BoxFace truth:
     Tier A, constant Cd=2.2      : final   -1638.8 km, max   1638.8 km
     Tier A, VariableCd.sphere    : final   -1426.4 km, max   1426.4 km
   (both use projected-area drag -> omit the large-face shear entirely)

2. Best-fit CONSTANT Cd (wide sweep) -- is the divergence absorbable, and
   at what Cd?
     Cd=  2.2: RMS along-track vs BoxFace     728.3 km
     Cd=  3.0: RMS along-track vs BoxFace     490.1 km
     Cd=  4.0: RMS along-track vs BoxFace     185.2 km
     Cd=  5.0: RMS along-track vs BoxFace     125.8 km  <- min
     Cd=  7.0: RMS along-track vs BoxFace     760.0 km
     Cd= 10.0: RMS along-track vs BoxFace    1710.2 km
     Cd= 15.0: RMS along-track vs BoxFace    3121.2 km
     best-fit c* = 4.74   (residual final 103.1 km, max 103.1 km)
     -> that is 2.2x the textbook 2.2, and ABOVE the free-molecular physical range (~2.0-2.6)
        i.e. no PHYSICALLY-reasoned constant Cd recovers BoxFace here

3. Does drag even matter for this sail? (drag vs SRP along-track effect)
     BoxFace drag effect (vs no-drag) : final    3006.4 km, max   3006.4 km
     Tier A(2.2) drag effect          : final    1445.9 km
     SRP effect (no-drag vs grav-only): final       0.6 km, max      0.7 km
     analytic SRP accel ~ 4.10e-06 m/s^2 (Cr~1.8, A/m=0.50)
     BoxFace drag effect / SRP effect = 4970.2x

----------------------------------------------------------------------------
HEADLINE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Empirical drag underestimate of physical Tier A vs BoxFace: ~2.1x
A user running physical Tier A (Cd=2.2 or VariableCd) mis-predicts the
along-track by ~1639 km over 5 days; recovering it needs Cd~4.7 (above the physical range).
(Not the idealized ~9x: InPlaneTracking tracks INERTIAL velocity, but the
 flow is Earth-relative (co-rotation), so the sail sits a few deg off exact
 edge-on -- the large faces keep some projected area Tier A does capture.)
At this altitude/epoch, BoxFace drag is 4970.2x the SRP effect, so drag DOMINATES SRP.
READING (NOT the decision -- the SHIP/DROP call is the maintainer's):
  Edge-on inverts the Sun-pointing result: the shear term is the dominant
  drag, projected-area Tier A structurally omits it, and no PHYSICAL
  constant Cd recovers it. If drag matters vs SRP here (see #3), this is
  BoxFaceCd's load-bearing use case.
