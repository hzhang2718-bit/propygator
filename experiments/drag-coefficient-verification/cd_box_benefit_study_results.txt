HEADLINE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Worst case (beta 30.0 deg): a best-fit CONSTANT Cd still
leaves 72.3 km of along-track divergence over 3 days,
= 1.12 % of the Tier-A drag effect itself (RMS 22.4 km).
This is the part NO constant Cd can absorb: the attitude-correlated
per-face shape that only BoxFaceCd represents.

READING (NOT the decision -- the SHIP/DROP call is the maintainer's):
  * residual that is a coherent, growing few-% of the drag effect
    and survives recalibration => MATERIAL => SHIP signal.
  * residual that collapses to ~0 at the best-fit Cd => DROP.
